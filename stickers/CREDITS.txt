Stickers for زينب تلعب وتتعلم

The glitter jelly squishies (pink, heart, cloud, bunny, star, rainbow, purple, mint, peach) are original SVG drawings made for this app (2026).

All other stickers are made from Microsoft Fluent Emoji 3D (https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji), MIT License,
upscaled with a white die-cut border added and, on the fruit and sweets, small kawaii faces drawn on.

Sounds (squeak, boing, pop, twinkle, page turn, breathing) are synthesised in the app with Web Audio; nothing is downloaded.
